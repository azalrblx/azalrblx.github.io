By joining the AZAL Roblox group, our Discord server or any AZAL flight, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not take part. Last updated 22 October 2026.

1. About AZAL

AZAL is a fan-run Roblox airline group inspired by Azerbaijan Airlines. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Azerbaijan Airlines CJSC or any real airline.

2. Who can join

  1. 2.1You must follow the Roblox Terms of Use and Discord Terms of Service, including their age requirements.
  2. 2.2One account per person. Alternate accounts used to dodge punishments, farm rewards or vote twice will be banned along with the main account.
  3. 2.3Your Roblox and Discord accounts must be linked through our verification bot to hold any rank above Economy Class.

3. Conduct

These rules apply in our Roblox games, our Discord server and anywhere you represent AZAL.

  1. 3.1Treat everyone with respect. No harassment, hate speech, discrimination or bullying.
  2. 3.2No NSFW, violent or shocking content in chat, names, avatars or images.
  3. 3.3No spamming, flooding, mass pinging or advertising without permission.
  4. 3.4No exploiting, cheating or using bugs to your advantage. Report bugs to staff instead.
  5. 3.5No impersonating staff, other members or real Azerbaijan Airlines employees.
  6. 3.6No sharing other people's personal information.
  7. 3.7Keep conversations in the right channels, and in English or Azerbaijani unless a channel says otherwise.
  8. 3.8No trolling or deliberately disrupting flights, events or trainings.

4. Flights and events

  1. 4.1Follow instructions from cabin crew, pilots and other staff during a flight. Their decision is final while the flight is running.
  2. 4.2Stay in your assigned seat and class. Entering a cabin you have not paid or ranked for is not allowed.
  3. 4.3Flight times are posted in the announcements channel. We may delay, change or cancel flights at any time.
  4. 4.4You may join a flight late, but you can be refused boarding once the doors close.
  5. 4.5You may record or stream flights, as long as you do not harass anyone in the video.

5. Purchases and classes

  1. 5.1Robux purchases (game passes, class upgrades, donations) are final. We do not give refunds.
  2. 5.2What each class includes is set out on the Classes page. Perks may change, be added or be removed at any time.
  3. 5.3A ban or punishment does not entitle you to a refund, or to keep perks during the punishment.
  4. 5.4All purchases also fall under the Roblox Terms of Use. Chargebacks or scams lead to a permanent ban.
  5. 5.5AZAL never sells or trades staff ranks.

6. Staff and ranks

  1. 6.1Staff ranks run from Personnel up to the Executive Council. Ranks are earned through applications, trainings and activity.
  2. 6.2No rank is guaranteed. Staff can be demoted or removed for inactivity, misconduct or breaking these terms.
  3. 6.3Staff must follow these terms. Abusing staff powers leads to removal and may lead to a ban.
  4. 6.4Decisions by the Directorial and Executive Councils are final, except through the appeal process in section 7.

7. Moderation and appeals

Punishments usually follow these steps. Staff can skip steps for serious breaks, such as hate speech, exploiting, scams or sharing personal information.

8. Privacy, content and changes

  1. 8.1We only keep what our bots need to run the group: your Roblox and Discord usernames and IDs, your rank and your moderation history. We never ask for passwords, real names or addresses.
  2. 8.2AZAL logos, emojis, banners and other designs belong to AZAL and may not be reused without permission.
  3. 8.3Azerbaijan Airlines names and marks belong to their owners. AZAL uses them only as a fan tribute, with no commercial claim.
  4. 8.4We may update these terms at any time. Changes are posted in the announcements channel, and staying in the group means you accept them.

Getting your class

  1. Join the AZAL Roblox group. You get Economy Class straight away.
  2. To upgrade, buy the Business Class game pass on our game page.
  3. Run the verify command in our Discord server. The bot gives you the matching role within a few minutes.

Conditions

  • Your class is tied to your Roblox account and cannot be transferred, gifted or sold.
  • Purchases are final, as set out in the Terms of Service.
  • Sitting in a cabin you have not paid for will get you moved. Repeated cases count as a rule break.
  • Classes are separate from staff ranks. Buying Business Class does not give any staff powers.

Requirements

  • Be an aviation group on Roblox: an airline, airport, alliance or aviation community.
  • Have at least 50 members in your Roblox group or Discord server.
  • Hold flights or events at least once a month.
  • Have a Discord server with clear rules and active moderation.
  • Follow the Roblox Terms of Use and Discord Terms of Service.
  • Not copy AZAL's, or any other group's, assets, branding or games.

Partners must not poach AZAL staff or members, or advertise outside the partners channel.

How to apply

  1. Open a partnership ticket in our support channel.
  2. Send your group link, Discord invite, member count and a short description of your group.
  3. The Managerial Council will reply within 7 days.
  4. If approved, both groups exchange representatives and add each other to their partner lists.

Either side can end a partnership at any time. AZAL will end it straight away if a partner breaks the Terms of Service, is inactive for 30 days or no longer meets the requirements.
