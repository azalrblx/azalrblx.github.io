AZAL is a place to fly, chat and take part in events in a friendly, respectful community. To keep it that way, we ask all members to follow the rules below. By taking part in AZAL flights, events, games or our Discord server, you agree to these Terms of Service.

Breaking these terms can lead to action decided by the AZAL Executive Council, including warnings, mutes, kicks, bans and blacklists. AZAL management may act against anyone whose behaviour harms the community or its reputation, even if that behaviour is not listed here.

AZAL is a fan-run Roblox group inspired by Azerbaijan Airlines and is not affiliated with Azerbaijan Airlines CJSC.

1. General rules

1.1 Violence and threats

  • Content showing violence, gore or animal abuse is not allowed.
  • Threats to harm anyone, physically, emotionally or financially, are not tolerated.

1.2 Bullying and harassment

  • Do not degrade, shame or harass anyone.
  • Discrimination or hate speech based on any of the following is not allowed: race, ethnicity or national origin; religion; gender identity or sexual orientation; age, disability or medical conditions.
  • Do not spam or share misleading content.

1.3 Harmful content

  • Content that glorifies or encourages self-harm, eating disorders or suicide is not allowed, and neither is encouraging anyone to harm themselves.

1.4 Privacy and impersonation

  • Do not share anyone's personal information, such as photos of faces, IDs or links to their other social media.
  • Do not impersonate anyone, including Personnel or real Azerbaijan Airlines employees.

1.5 Illegal behaviour

  • Anything illegal, including fraud, offering or asking for sexual services, or exploiting minors in any way, is not allowed. It leads to immediate action and may be reported to the authorities.

1.6 Sexual content

  • Sexual or explicit content is not allowed. Any content sexualising minors leads to a permanent blacklist and a report to the relevant authorities.

2. Roblox rules

  1. 2.1Follow instructions from Personnel during a flight. Their decision is final while the flight is running.
  2. 2.2Do not try to get into restricted areas, including by using glitches or following Personnel. Report glitches instead.
  3. 2.3Do not use Personnel-only equipment.
  4. 2.4Stay in your class. Do not sit in, or use the services of, a class you have not bought.
  5. 2.5Do not beg for upgrades.
  6. 2.6No trolling or disrupting flights.
  7. 2.7No exploiting, under any circumstances.
  8. 2.8No terrorism or other offensive roleplay.
  9. 2.9Do not bypass the chat filter or spam the chat.
  10. 2.10Inappropriate avatars or clothing are not allowed, and you may be asked to change before boarding. AZAL management can decide an avatar is inappropriate even if it is not covered here.
  11. 2.11Once a flight's server locks, it cannot be joined.
  12. 2.12You must follow the Roblox Terms of Use.

3. Discord rules

  1. 3.1Use each channel for what it is meant for.
  2. 3.2Do not ping people without a reason, including ghost pings. For questions, open a ticket in the support channel.
  3. 3.3Do not play distorted, annoying or very loud sounds in voice channels.
  4. 3.4Do not misuse bots.
  5. 3.5Do not mention leaks, exploits or anything else malicious.
  6. 3.6Do not start or add to drama.
  7. 3.7Political and religious discussions are not allowed.
  8. 3.8English and Azerbaijani are the only languages allowed.
  9. 3.9You must be at least 13 to join the AZAL Discord server.
  10. 3.10You must follow the Discord Terms of Service.

1. Ümumi qaydalar

1.1 Zorakılıq və hədələr

  • Zorakılıq, qan və ya heyvanlara qarşı qəddarlıq əks etdirən məzmun qadağandır.
  • Kiməsə fiziki, emosional və ya maddi zərər vurmaqla hədələmək yolverilməzdir.

1.2 Zorbalıq və təhqir

  • Heç kimi alçaltmayın, utandırmayın və təhqir etməyin.
  • Aşağıdakılara əsaslanan ayrı-seçkilik və ya nifrət nitqi qadağandır: irq, etnik və ya milli mənşə; din; gender kimliyi və ya cinsi oriyentasiya; yaş, əlillik və ya sağlamlıq vəziyyəti.
  • Spam etməyin və yanıltıcı məzmun paylaşmayın.

1.3 Zərərli məzmun

  • Özünə zərər vurmağı, qidalanma pozuntularını və ya intiharı tərifləyən və ya təşviq edən məzmun, eləcə də kimisə özünə zərər vurmağa təşviq etmək qadağandır.

1.4 Məxfilik və təqlid

  • Heç kimin şəxsi məlumatlarını, məsələn, üz şəkillərini, sənədlərini və ya digər sosial şəbəkə linklərini paylaşmayın.
  • Personal (Personnel) və ya real Azərbaycan Hava Yolları əməkdaşları da daxil olmaqla, heç kimi təqlid etməyin.

1.5 Qanunsuz davranış

  • Fırıldaqçılıq, cinsi xidmət təklif etmək və ya istəmək, yaxud azyaşlıları hər hansı formada istismar etmək daxil olmaqla, istənilən qanunsuz fəaliyyət qadağandır. Bu, dərhal tədbir görülməsinə səbəb olur və müvafiq orqanlara bildirilə bilər.

1.6 Cinsi məzmun

  • Cinsi və ya açıq-saçıq məzmun qadağandır. Azyaşlıları seksuallaşdıran istənilən məzmun daimi qara siyahıya salınma və müvafiq orqanlara məlumat verilməsi ilə nəticələnir.

Is AZAL the real Azerbaijan Airlines?
No. AZAL is a fan-run Roblox group inspired by Azerbaijan Airlines and has no link to the real airline.
How do I fly with AZAL?
Join our Roblox group and Discord server. Flight times are posted in the announcements channel, so join the game before the server locks.
When are flights?
Every flight is announced in the announcements channel. Turn on notifications for that channel so you don't miss one.
I missed a flight because the server locked. Can I still join?
No. Once a server locks, it can't be joined. Look out for the next flight.
How do I get Business Class?
Buy the Business Class game pass on our game page. See the Classes page for what's included.
Can I get a refund?
No. All Robux purchases are final.
How do I report someone?
Open a ticket in the support channel of our Discord server, with screenshots or a clip if you have them.
I was punished and think it was unfair. What can I do?
Open a ticket in the support channel and explain what happened. We'll take another look.
How can I join the AZAL team?
When applications open, we post them in the announcements channel.
Can my group partner with AZAL?
Open a partnership ticket in our Discord server.
What languages can I use?
English or Azerbaijani, unless a channel says otherwise.
